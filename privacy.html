Privacy policy

homelab rclone is a personal backup tool used only by Martin, for his own home server.

  • Who uses it: only Martin, with his own Google account.
  • What it accesses: only the files it creates itself in that Google Drive (Google's drive.file permission). It cannot see any other files.
  • What it stores there: encrypted backup files. They are encrypted before they leave the home server, so Google stores only unreadable data.
  • Sharing: no data is shared with, sold to or seen by anyone else. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Removal: access can be withdrawn at any time in the Google account's security settings, and the backup files deleted from Drive.

Contact: the owner of the GitHub account that publishes this page.
